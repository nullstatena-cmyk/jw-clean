What moves the number. Pane count per unit is the biggest factor, followed by access. Ground-floor units with clear approach price at the low end. Second-floor glass, landscaping in the way, gated breezeways, or anything needing an escort moves it up.

Volume and frequency move it down. A 240-unit property on a quarterly common-area schedule with steady make-ready flow prices meaningfully better than one-off work.

Terms. Net 30 from invoice date. One invoice per property per month, with make-readies itemised by unit number. No mobilisation fee, no fuel surcharge, no minimum for properties inside the service area.
