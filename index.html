What we do

Two kinds of work, both sized for a single operator.

We are deliberately narrow. Ground-level glass, done well, scheduled reliably, invoiced on net 30.

Make-ready turns

Per unit · scheduled around your turn board

Interior and exterior glass on vacant units, cleaned between move-out and move-in. Tracks and sills wiped, stickers and paint overspray removed.

  • Priced per unit, not per hour
  • Batch several units in one visit
  • Photos on completion if your process needs them

Recurring common areas

Monthly or quarterly · fixed visit rate

The glass residents and prospects actually look through. Leasing office, clubhouse, fitness center, mail room, breezeway and stairwell windows.

  • Same day each cycle, so you can forget about it
  • Worked outside leasing hours where access allows
  • One invoice per property, per month

Storefront

Retail strips · weekly to monthly

If you manage a mixed-use or retail component, ground-floor storefront glass runs on the same route and the same invoice.

  • Early morning before open
  • Per-pane or per-visit pricing
  • Easy add to an existing common-area contract
Why pole-fed matters to you

The reason we do not own a ladder.

Lower risk on site

No ladder against your siding, no worker overhead, no cones blocking a breezeway. The most common source of vendor incident reports simply is not present.

In and out quietly

One operator, a bucket and a pole. No crew staging in your parking lot, no equipment left on site overnight, no residents asking the office what the noise is.

Honest about the limit

We reach roughly two stories from grade. If your building is taller than that, we will tell you on the call rather than after the bid, and we are happy to hand you a referral.
